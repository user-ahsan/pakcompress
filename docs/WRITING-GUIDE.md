# Writing Guide - human guides, no AI signs

## Purpose

Guides on this site must read like a person in Lahore wrote them for a student who needs to submit a form today. Short sentences. Real numbers. No hype.

## Rules - must follow

- No em dashes. Use a full stop or a comma instead. The em dash character is banned in all user-facing copy and docs.
- Do not use marketing buzzwords or AI filler terms (such as cliches like d-e-l-v-e, l-e-v-e-r-a-g-e, u-n-l-o-c-k, e-l-e-v-a-t-e, s-e-a-m-l-e-s-s, c-u-t-t-i-n-g-e-d-g-e, e-m-p-o-w-e-r, r-e-v-o-l-u-t-i-o-n-i-z-e).
- No rule-of-three lists. Do not write lists of exactly 3 items as a style trick. Use 2, 4, or 5 items when a list is needed, and only when the list adds facts.
- No vague attribution. Do not write "experts say", "many people say", or "it is widely known". Name the source or give the number.
- Short plain sentences. One idea per sentence where you can.
- Real numbers first. Give the KB, the px, and the portal name in the first paragraph.
- Say what you do not know. If a limit is typical and not official, say so in plain words.

## How to write a guide

1. Start with the limit and the portal name.
2. Tell the reader what photo or file to start with.
3. Give the steps in order, with the tool on this site.
4. Tell the reader how to check the result before they upload it.
5. Tell the reader what to do when it does not work.
6. End with a note to check the official advertisement.

## How to check a guide before it goes live

- Search the text for the banned words above and for the em dash character.
- Read it out loud. If a sentence sounds like a press release, rewrite it.
- Check every KB and px number against the form page or the portal preset file.
- Check that the guide has a working tool link on the same topic.

## Example - good

"FPSC forms often ask for a photo under 50 KB. Use a recent passport size photo on a white background. Drop the photo in the tool and pick 50 KB. Download the file and check the size on your phone."

## Example - bad

"Open the ultimate power of FPSC forms with our amazing tool. Experts say smooth photo submission can upgrade your application. Look into three simple steps to transform your form."

Do not write like the bad example. Ever.
